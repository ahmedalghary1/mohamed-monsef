# Creative Performance — November 2026 redesign

The owner’s latest supplied brief replaces the original light-only portfolio direction. The result pairs a deep navy opening with spacious light editorial sections, a dark motion break and a navy closing contact area. Cyan is concentrated on dark surfaces; deep cyan carries accessible text on light.

The actual supplied photograph anchors the hero in a slightly angled editorial frame. Its original white/yellow background is intentionally retained rather than attempting a poor cutout or reconstructing the face. The composition preserves the smile, hairstyle, clothing and crossed arms. The desktop portrait occupies roughly 44% of the two-column opening; mobile presents the heading, CTAs and then the portrait. Arabic mirrors the reading flow through logical CSS, without reversing the photograph.

Space Grotesk, Inter and IBM Plex Sans Arabic are local licensed WOFF2 fonts. A custom geometric MM is used in the header, footer, portrait detail, favicon and sharing image. Tokens live in `src/styles/tokens.css`. The supplied reference artwork informs hierarchy and composition only and is never published as Mohamed’s work.

The owner-requested logo refresh replaces the thin zigzag construction with two filled, clearly separated M letterforms. Matching stem widths, open inner spaces and complementary clipped corners define the mark. The name uses a compact two-line lockup in the header/footer. `src/data/brand.json` owns its geometry; inline SVG, light/dark/cyan exports, transparent PNG, favicon and social image all use those same polygons. The mark itself does not depend on fonts or external resources.

Open industry rows communicate real estate, retail and dates/agri-food experience without suggesting unpublished case studies. The image lab has a tall product feature and two offset studies, with categories, native project dialogs and explicit coding-assistant provenance. Its generated artwork remains separate from approved real-project data. The dark motion area plays a genuinely encoded silent study, labelled as programmed animation rather than AI video. Service rows, a four-step connected process, monogram-led about section, compact career timeline and grouped toolkit complete the page.

GSAP provides finite desktop entrances, image transforms and process-line progress; Lenis uses one GSAP ticker. Mobile uses native scrolling and a short transform entrance, without downloading GSAP/Lenis. Reduced-motion users receive the static presentation. Scroll-entering text retains full contrast before activation. The portrait does not move continuously. Three.js is absent from the active bundle.

The contact section remains a preview until direct channels are approved. No fake availability, metrics, testimonials, clients, form submissions or generated likenesses are used. Real work, public CV/contact and canonical/domain settings are all owner-controlled gates. Static metadata and both language routes exist independently of JS.

Visual and technical evidence is in `docs/redesign-verification.md`; the earlier `docs/verification.md` and old screenshots are historical. No publishing, source upload or Git commit has been performed.
